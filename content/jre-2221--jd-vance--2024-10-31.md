Vance criticizes U.S. foreign policy since the Cold War, especially the Iraq War, arguing that it destabilized the Middle East and empowered Iran. He contends that corporations and Democrats promote lax immigration policies for cheap labor and to shift electoral demographics. Vance and Rogan argue that voter ID laws are necessary to prevent election fraud, and they claim that opposition to these laws is based on a patronizing view of minority communities. Vance expresses cautious optimism for Trump’s election chances but warns of possible electoral manipulation, urging strong voter turnout to counteract it.
