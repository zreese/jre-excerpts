gist: RFK Jr. claims in this interview that vaccines—especially those administered since the 1986 liability shield—have caused a massive rise in chronic diseases, including autism, ADHD, autoimmune disorders, and allergies. He argues that many vaccines were never safety-tested in placebo-controlled trials and cites the 1986 National Childhood Vaccine Injury Act as enabling unchecked expansion of the vaccine schedule. He suggests links between early-life vaccination and neurodevelopmental harm, relying heavily on anecdotal cases and discredited or controversial studies, such as the Verstraeten study and the Simpsonwood meeting. He states that aluminum in vaccines causes allergies and neurotoxicity, and that glyphosate and WiFi radiation contribute to modern health problems. He revives the claim that the Spanish flu was bacterial and possibly vaccine-induced—a view refuted by mainstream historians and epidemiologists. He misrepresents studies like Lazarus (2010) and the Cleveland Clinic COVID vaccine study, suggesting negative efficacy without adequate context. He also falsely claims Ivermectin was suppressed to allow vaccine emergency use authorization, alleging that studies were intentionally designed to fail. Many of these statements—especially those linking vaccines to autism, claiming vaccine trials lacked placebo groups, and asserting widespread health damage from 5G/WiFi—are either heavily disputed or outright debunked by consensus scientific evidence. The episode’s viral moments include his assertions about “good mercury vs. bad mercury,” false vaccine-efficacy math, and censorship of his podcast appearances, such as deletions from YouTube (e.g., Theo Von and Mike Tyson episodes).
