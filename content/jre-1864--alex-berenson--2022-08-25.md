gist: Alex Berenson discusses his suspension and reinstatement on Twitter, claiming he was banned for tweeting that COVID vaccines don’t stop infection or transmission and suggesting they should be treated like therapeutics. He explains that he sued Twitter and won a partial legal victory, obtaining internal communications that, according to him, show the Biden White House pressured Twitter to ban him. He argues this violated his First Amendment rights and plans to sue the federal government and specific officials. Berenson accuses media, tech platforms, and public health institutions of coordinated censorship, influenced by political motives and corporate interests. He claims YouTube and other platforms later revised their content moderation policies to allow statements he was initially banned for. He suggests there has been a rise in all-cause mortality and a drop in birth rates in heavily vaccinated countries and proposes that the COVID vaccines may be responsible, though he admits other factors could be involved. He criticizes the suppression of discussions around vaccine side effects and says medical and media institutions have been captured by ideological and financial interests. Berenson maintains that even if vaccines worked, the coercion, censorship, and demonization of dissent were unjustified. He also claims internal Slack messages from Twitter suggest the White House explicitly asked why Berenson was still allowed to tweet, supporting his censorship claims.
