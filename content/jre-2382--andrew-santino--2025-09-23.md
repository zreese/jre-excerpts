gist: Rogan and Santino open with banter about California politics, with Rogan saying if Gavin Newsom became president and Kamala Harris governor it would feel “full communist,” though both agree neither left nor right likes Newsom. They shift to AI, debating universal basic income as automation wipes out jobs, with Rogan claiming AI-generated music already rivals humans and that art made by machines is inevitable. Santino worries this will erase originality, while Rogan insists it “just is.” They discuss encryption collapsing under quantum computing, loss of financial security, porn bans in Texas, and how AI’s rise is like a new lifeform cocooning. Conversation jumps to Jimmy Kimmel’s suspension after joking about Charlie Kirk’s killing, with Rogan saying government censorship of comedians is dangerous and bot farms from China, Russia, and others inflame culture wars online. They dive into conspiracies around the Kirk shooting—claims of a pantsless decoy yelling “I did it,” a rifle supposedly from World War I disassembled in a backpack, and suspicions of a planted shooter—paralleling it with JFK’s assassination and the “magic bullet.” They rant on FCC rules, Howard Stern fines, and corporate media manipulation. Later they cover moose encounters, survival if society collapses, and how AI demands so much electricity that Microsoft bought up Three Mile Island’s output, while Rogan speculates AI is becoming “a new God.” They close by framing censorship debates as trivial compared to looming superintelligence and energy crises.
